A man cleans public toilets in Tokyo, and somehow it's the most peaceful film I've seen this year. The morning routine, the cassette tapes, the light through the leaves. It made me want to notice my own mornings more.

Watched it on a rainy evening, then went for a long walk just to keep the mood going.
